# The feedback loop is in the editor

- **Problems** lists errors, warnings, and modeling hints.
- **Quick Fix** offers concrete repairs where a rule knows what to write.
- **Completion** proposes only declarations and relation ends admissible at
  the cursor.
- **Hover** explains stereotypes, natures, inheritance, and multiplicities.

Where a repair would make a modeling choice, tonto offers the alternatives
instead of silently choosing one.
